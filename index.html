YardContract is a service agreement builder made for independent lawn care operators running roughly 10–50 recurring properties — the ones still pricing from gut feel, handshake deals, and spreadsheets. Price every lawn by its real size, count the season's visits on the real calendar, choose per-visit or flat-rate billing, and see your true profit on the whole contract — then put it all in a written agreement your client signs. No more "can you also trim the hedge?" for free, no more arguments about what was included.

YardContract is deliberately not a Jobber or LMN replacement: no scheduling, no invoicing, no CRM. Just profitable pricing and professional agreements, without the complicated software.

How it works

1

Measure the yard

Enter the lot size and YardContract picks the right price tier. Add flat surcharges for wide gates, slopes, and obstacle-heavy yards — the things that make a "simple" lawn take twice as long.

2

Build the season

Pick weekly, every-2-weeks, or monthly visits and set the real season start and end dates — visits are counted on the actual calendar, not a rough month estimate. Add seasonal services like fertilization, aeration, and leaf cleanup, then choose how the client pays: per visit, equal monthly payments during the season, or equal monthly payments all year.

3

Sign it, then mow

Download a clean agreement with the scope, schedule, prices, billing plan, weather and cancellation terms, and signature lines for both of you. Your crew costs and margin stay private — the client sees services and prices only. Next season, renew every client in one click with your new prices.

Per visit, seasonal flat rate (equal payments during the season), or annual flat rate (equal payments all year) — you pick. Flat-rate payments are rounded to the cent and the final payment absorbs the remainder, so installments always add up to the exact season total.

The fine print, in plain words
